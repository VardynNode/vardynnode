## What's new

- **Chart button.** Each grid and position card has a Chart button. It shows the price of that coin or stock over 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, with your buys and sells marked on it. It is closed until you open it.
- **Stocks (Experimental) on the test exchange.** You can try stocks with practice money before you use real money.
- **Stocks beside your coins.** Stocks are listed beside your coins everywhere (Grid, Holdings, account totals, Telegram). A stock that cannot start now says why.
- **Out-of-range grid.** A grid whose price has left its range now extends or re-centers instead of waiting.
- **Internal builds.** 8.0.1 and 8.0.2 were internal builds and were not released; their changes are included here.
