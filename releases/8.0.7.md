## What's new

- **Stocks start reliably after a restart.** The bot reads your list of stocks correctly, and if a stock cannot be started yet it says why and tries again by itself. A running stock grid is no longer stopped at a restart as if its coin had been removed from your list, and the portfolio check names a running stock grid as a grid.
- **Budgets kept apart.** Stock grids and coin grids keep their budgets apart: raising a stock's budget is checked against the stock total only, raising a coin's against the coin total only, and a stock grid is never resized to a coin share.
- **Saved grids that are not running.** When a saved grid is not running (for example Stocks is switched off, or a stock is not confirmed yet), its waiting buy orders are cancelled at start so nothing buys without a grid to sell it. Its sell orders and saved state are kept, nothing is sold, and saved stock grids resume by themselves once the stock is confirmed.
- **Waiting to start.** A saved grid that has to wait to start (for free cash, or while another grid finishes switching to a new budget) now says so once, in plain words, and starts by itself as soon as it can.
- **Internal builds.** 8.0.4, 8.0.5 and 8.0.6 were internal builds (not released); their changes are included here.
