## What's new

**New in 8.0.16**

- **A grid order Binance can't accept right now is no longer sent.** Before each buy or sell level of a grid, the bot checks the price band Binance allows around the 5-minute average price. An order outside the band waits quietly and says so once, for example: "the buy at 123.06 is too far below the market for Binance right now (it accepts 123.71 and up); it is placed when the price comes nearer." No retry every minute, no failed-order rows for it and no "stuck" alert. If the average price can't be read, nothing is blocked (and if Binance itself refuses a price, the bot does not send it again for 5 minutes). A sale at the grid's floor price that falls outside the band is not sent either; the grid then says it did not fill, which is not the clearest wording, and a clearer one comes in the next build.
- **A grid stopped by the safety stop no longer says "was removed but is acting again" every hour.** Only a real order action by a stopped grid raises it now, and the alert clears by itself.
- **A stopped grid's card says it is stopped**, not running: "Stopped by the safety stop on <date>; your coins are kept in Held. Start it again from Grid settings when you want."
- **The short AI questions are no longer cut off.** The market-mood question, the manual sell and buy checks, the AI test and the lot advice could come back empty ("No text"), because the model used its whole short answer to think. For Claude Haiku the thinking is now switched off for these calls; for Sonnet and Opus the effort is set to low. The longer answers (Explain, and a question you ask in Telegram) are not changed, and a dated or alias model name does not get the fix yet. Watch the log after you update.
- **License clean-up.** The trial check's timer uses the computer's steady clock, and code that could never run is removed. Nothing changes in how the license works for you.

**From 8.0.15**

- **A grid that only sells now says why it does not move.** This is a grid of the old currency winding down after a currency switch, or a grid you drained. When there is a loss it shows what it holds (quantity, today's price, cost, worth now and loss, in the grid's own currency and coin); it says that it waits for its sell prices and that you can use Sell now to sell anyway. It no longer says "will resume" when it will not.
- **Such a grid is no longer paused at a range break**, and it no longer sends the Telegram message "Auto-resumes when price returns".
- **The grid card uses the grid's own currency and coin.** A USDC grid on a USDT account showed USDT before; it now shows USDC, and the coin name is the coin, not the pair.
- **No profit estimate for a grid that places no new buys.**
- **Coin kept aside on a winding-down grid is explained**: it was bought in an earlier, higher range, above this grid's top price, so no level of the grid sells it above its cost.

**From 8.0.14**

- **The risk check says what it could not read.** For example "the records of ETHUSDT could not be read" or "your USDC balance could not be read yet (your currency switch is still winding down)", instead of a general message. The check also reads the old currency's cash more reliably at start-up.
- **An old-currency grid is left alone while a switch winds down.** It is no longer compared with your new settings at every start; for a real coin grid the message says plainly that nothing was changed and how to use the new setting (Reset & Rebuild on that grid, which sells the coin it holds).
- **Plain words on the start and status lines**: the Telegram start message, the /status mode line, the System Health mode line, the learning-filter training message, the signal lines and the past-prices test messages.
- **The market mood number now has the right sign.** Fear & Greed 12 (extreme fear) used to count as a positive mood ("Bullish"); it now counts as negative ("Bearish"). **Money effect (Smart DCA only):** it counts fear as fear, so on its 0-to-1 fear score it tops up a bit more in fear markets (up by 0.04 to 0.13) and a bit less in greed markets (down by 0.01 to 0.12); at Fear & Greed 50 nothing changes. The AI read is shown in the log line but is not part of the number.
- **No more "Grid data error" on the dashboard** for grids that are paused or winding down and out of range.

**From 8.0.13**

- **Money settings are logged.** Every change of a money setting writes a line (name, old value, new value; keys, tokens, passwords and PINs are named without values). Three amounts were missing from that list and now ask for your PIN or 2FA code like the other money settings: Stocks Total Allocation, TradingView Order Size - Mode and TradingView Order Size - Value.
- **Plain words on the /settings, /mode and /coins messages.**
- **The stock line shows which stocks run**, never "N of the plan limit".
- **A change of the stock total takes effect at once.** It is refused if it would make a stock order smaller than the exchange minimum, and the waiting buys of running stock grids are re-sized.
- **A leftover stock you move by hand goes to its own stock grid.**
- **The Directional strategy no longer waits for the conviction rating** before its next step.
- **Old names are dropped** (the old cookie names and the old browser-storage move). If you update from 8.0.7 or later nothing changes for you; if you come from an older build you sign in once more.

**From 8.0.12**

- **A refused Adopt or Reassign of a held coin now says why.** The message used to read "was refused (None)"; it now gives the real reason.
