## What's new

- **An update no longer waits just because you hold positions.** On a live bot it waits only while something is at risk right now, for example an order being placed, a price within 2% of a stop, or a sell that Smart TP has taken off the exchange while it follows the price. It looks again every 2 minutes and installs as soon as that clears, at any hour. After about 24 hours of waiting it installs anyway and says why. Automatic updates must be on for this; the command to update at once is unchanged.
- **You can see what an update waits for.** The Support tab and the update log say the reason in plain words.
- **A sell that Binance refuses because of its price limit no longer counts toward pausing a grid.** The bot treats it as a wait and sends the sell when the price comes nearer.
- **When the Smart TP exit cannot be sent because of that limit, the bot says so**, once, instead of "did not fill".
- **A removed grid whose warning ends now says it is quiet and stays stopped.** Before, the log said it was placing orders again.
- **Short AI questions keep working with dated versions of the same AI model.**
- **The buttons on a grid card handle the coin's name more safely.**
