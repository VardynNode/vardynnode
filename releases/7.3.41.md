## What's new

- **No false holdings warning right after a sale.** The holdings list and the bot's total now read the same wallet reading, so a sale that lands in between no longer causes a warning in the log. A real, unexplained gap still warns as before.
- **A grid below its range recenters when its own budget is used up**, instead of waiting for an extend that cannot run. When the recenter has to wait, the log says so once.
