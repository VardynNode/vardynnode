## What's new

- **How many stocks your plan runs follows your main strategy.** Stocks count like coins: Pro runs 5 stocks (1 while the grid is your main strategy), Elite runs 10 (5 while the grid is your main strategy), Starter has none, and a trial runs 1 on paper only. Check my settings tells you before you save if a change of main strategy would leave you with more stocks than the plan allows.
- **Stocks (Experimental).** A new switch in Bot Config, off by default. It runs a grid on US stocks, traded as Binance bStocks (a token backed by the share, not the share itself), beside your coins. Stocks have their own list and their own budget; they use no coin slot and no grid slot. Your plan sets how many stocks you can add (the trial runs them on paper only).
- **US market hours.** Around the US open and close the bot pauses stock buys (from 15 minutes before to 60 minutes after, both numbers are settings). Waiting buys are cancelled and come back when the pause ends; sells stay.
- **Care with leveraged funds.** Some bStocks are leveraged or inverse funds. Check what a token is before you add it.
- **A stock token is never a coin.** The bot refuses a stock token in the coin lists.
- **Grid fixes.** A grid that starts before its first price has arrived now waits for the price instead of logging errors. In paper mode a waiting buy is no longer booked as nothing bought. A stock grid that cannot start now says why. The time shown by the Coin Scanner follows your own time zone.
