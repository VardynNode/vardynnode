## What's new

- **Switching between USDT and USDC is smoother.** While your old grids wind down, their coin is no longer reported as not managed, and the portfolio check no longer says it differs. The money the old grids are not using is converted right away, and you can choose to convert your stock budget with the switch. The Holdings page shows the whole wallet, including the coin still being sold, and the card of a winding-down grid offers only what such a grid can do.
- **Total Allocation.** The setting now says how it is shared (across your grid coins first, then across each grid's levels) and shows what one grid gets.
- **Telegram in plain words.** /status is in four groups (Money, What is running, Market mood, Health). The daily report, the detail commands, /settings and /set read the same way.
- **The grid backtest follows the live grid.** A level that sold is bought again when the price falls back through it, the buys stay within the grid's capital, a stock holds its buys around the US open and close, a buy cannot sell on its own candle, the band width follows Auto-Range, and there is no stop-loss unless one is asked for, because the live grid has none. A grid backtest result shows fewer trades than before.
- **Live grid chart.** The chart no longer draws a red zone below the range.
- **Internal builds.** 8.0.8 and 8.0.9 were internal builds (not released); their changes are included here.
