## What's new

- **Two license-key forms.** A license key can start with `AQP-` (the original form, four groups of four) or `VDN-` (a new, longer form, six groups of four). Both are valid forever; if your key starts with `AQP-`, nothing changes for you. A key typed in a wrong shape is refused with a plain sentence that names both forms.
- **Keys are shown short.** The license card and Telegram show a key as its first group and its last four characters (for example `VDN-1A2B...9F3C`), the same for both forms. The file you can make for support shows only the first group.
- **Plain messages about your license.** When it applies, the bot now tells you in plain words that your license is registered to a different Binance account (live trading pauses; paper and testnet trading and the dashboard keep working, and live trading resumes by itself, within about five minutes, once the license and the account match again), or that a free trial has already been used from your network, Binance account or server.
- **Recent Trades.** Every fill that has a real Binance order shows that order number first, once. A sale the bot recovered after a lost reply no longer shows the number twice.
- **A later part of a buy order.** When a later part of a grid buy waits to join the sell its first part has, the log says so in one plain line (information only; your coin is safe and nothing is lost).
